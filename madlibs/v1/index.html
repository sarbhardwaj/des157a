A Day at UC Davis Gone Wrong

It was a beautiful day at UC Davis. My alarm clock was replaced by a potato. I scrambled to get my backpack and hopped on my bicycle to class.

I showed up 25 minutes late to class. I walked into the classroom and found that my professor was replaced by a cow. No one seemed to care so I took a seat and continued acting as if nothing had happened.

My professor then leaped onto the desk and exclaimed, "pizza!" All the students in the classroom began dancing. I decided to exit the classroom.

I ran out into the library but the whole campus was filled with ducks. A student tossed me a burrito. I grabbed an umbrella to protect myself.

Things couldn't get any more chaotic until Chancellor May rode out on a huge horse blasting music.

Chancellor May then turned towards me and yelled, "Run away!"

I then turned around and headed back to my dorm. I ordered ramen. I had had my fill of UC Davis for one day.
